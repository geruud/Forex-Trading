2. How the trade logic works

Every 5 minutes, when a bar closes, the EA builds that bar's footprint in 0.40-dollar price rows, then:

1. Looks for a setup.
   - Exhaustion (almost all trades). The bar makes a new 20-bar low, but selling dries up at the bottom. The lowest row holds 3% or less of the bar's activity, selling gets smaller row by row towards the low, and the bar closes in its upper 60%. The idea: sellers ran out, so buy. Shorts are the mirror image at a 20-bar high.
   - Zone retest (about one a month). Three or more stacked buy imbalances form a zone. Price comes back into it within 30 bars, buyers defend it, and the 1-hour trend agrees. It enters with a market order.
   - Absorption. Almost never fires on this broker's data.
2. Scores the signal from 0 to 5 points, one each for:
   - the 1-hour trend agreeing;
   - being near a key level;
   - a strong imbalance at the extreme;
   - a recent delta divergence;
   - normal volatility.

   It needs at least 1 point.
3. Filters. It needs:
   - a spread of at most 0.15 × ATR;
   - at least 60 ticks in the bar;
   - no runaway volatility;
   - no high-impact news within 15 minutes;
   - not after 18:00 on Friday;
   - only one trade at a time.
4. Entry. A buy-stop at the signal bar's high, valid for 10 minutes. It's cancelled if price breaks the bar's low first. The stop goes just below the bar's low, and the target is 2R (twice the risk).
5. Management.
   - At +1R it closes half and moves the stop to break-even.
   - From +1.5R it trails the stop 1 ATR behind price.
   - After 1 hour, it closes the trade if it isn't at least +0.3R.
6. Risk.
   - Each trade risks 0.5% of equity.
   - At most 10 trades a day, and it stops for the day at −2%.
   - After 3 losses in a row, it pauses 60 minutes.
   - At a 15% drawdown it closes everything and stops for good.

docs\TRADE_LOGIC.md has the full version with a real worked trade.

3. Getting 5–6 trades a day

The original settings lost most signals to the 08:00–20:00 session filter and the grade-B minimum. Of 1,296 signals in June–August, only 13 became trades. I opened up those two gates (all-day session, grade C with at least 1 point) and raised the daily cap from 4 to 10. A 60-minute pause after 3 losses in a row now replaces "stop for the day".
